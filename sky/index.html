About the data

Stars: Hipparcos catalog positions, magnitudes and colors for the 25,700 stars brighter than magnitude 7.5, with names and Bayer and Flamsteed designations, from the open d3-celestial data set by Olaf Frohn.

Constellations: the 88 IAU constellations, their traditional stick figures and their official boundaries. Deep sky: the Messier catalog, galaxies brighter than magnitude 11 and other bright clusters and nebulae. Milky Way outline from the same data set.

Coordinates are right ascension and declination for the year 2000.
