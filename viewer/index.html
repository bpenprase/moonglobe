Background removal

Fits the sky's background, such as vignetting or a glow from light pollution, and subtracts it so the sky is even and close to zero.
A smooth surface suits gentle gradients across the frame. For round vignetting with a sharp edge, like the NGC 253 sample, choose the circular pattern.
Each point samples the sky in the square around it. Click a point to leave it out (for example one on a galaxy or a bright star), click it again to bring it back, or click empty sky to add a point.
Finds the center of a circular pattern and measures the sky in thin rings around it, so it can follow a sharp-edged vignetting disk. Stars and small galaxies barely affect the rings.
Smoothing (pixels)
How far along the radius the sky levels are smoothed. Larger values give a smoother background without circular ripples. Smaller values follow a sharp vignetting edge more closely.

Measure distances

In Measure mode, click one spot and then another. The line between them is measured in arcseconds using the plate scale. Dragging still moves the image. Esc cancels a half-finished line.
About this viewer

Plate scales come from the Nieves Observatory imaging table (1 × 1 binning): 0.303″ per pixel for the ASI6200MM Pro and ASI2600MC Pro on the PlaneWave CDK14 (2,563 mm, f/7.2), 0.233″ for the ASI585MC Pro on the CDK14, and 1.436″ for the ASI2600MC Pro on the 11-inch HyperStar v4 (540 mm, f/1.9). Binning multiplies the scale. A plate-solved frame gives the definitive value.

Pixel positions: for FITS images, x and y follow the FITS convention, counting from 1 at the lower left, and the image is shown with row 1 at the bottom. For JPG and PNG images, x and y count from 0 at the upper left. RA and Dec use the image's own WCS (gnomonic projection, with SIP distortion terms when present).

Samples: NGC 253 from the Nieves Observatory HyperStar system. The Horsehead Nebula FITS image is from the Digitized Sky Survey (STScI, UK Schmidt plate), as distributed with the astropy tutorials.

Everything runs in your browser. Images are not uploaded anywhere.
