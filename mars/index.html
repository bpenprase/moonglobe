About the data

Color: Viking Orbiter MDIM 2.1 colorized global mosaic (USGS / NASA Ames), about 232 m per pixel in the original.

Infrared: Mars Odyssey THEMIS daytime infrared global mosaic (NASA/JPL/Arizona State University), about 100 m per pixel. Warm, sunlit slopes look bright, so it shows the shape of the land very clearly.

Elevation: Mars Global Surveyor MOLA laser altimeter (NASA/GSFC), resampled to 8 pixels per degree. Heights are relative to the MOLA areoid, the Martian equivalent of sea level.

Names: IAU Gazetteer of Planetary Nomenclature (USGS). Maps and close-ups come from the NASA Mars Trek tile service.

Longitudes are east positive (planetocentric).
