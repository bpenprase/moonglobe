About the data

Surface: LRO Camera (LROC) Wide Angle Camera global mosaic, NASA/GSFC/Arizona State University, via the NASA Scientific Visualization Studio CGI Moon Kit. About 1.3 km per pixel at the equator.

Elevation: Lunar Orbiter Laser Altimeter (LOLA) gridded topography, resampled to 8 pixels per degree. Heights are relative to a 1737.4 km sphere.

Names: IAU Gazetteer of Planetary Nomenclature (USGS). Lunar 100 list by Charles Wood (Sky & Telescope, 2004).

Longitudes are east positive. Lunar near side faces you at the start.
